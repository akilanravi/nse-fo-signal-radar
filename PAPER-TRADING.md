# Nightly F&O paper replay

Add only `paper_replay.py` at the root of `nse-fo-signal-radar` and `.github/workflows/paper-trading.yml` at the stated workflow path. Do not replace any current scanner, morning watchlist, or app files. Reuses existing Dhan secrets and `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN_1` / `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID_1`. No real orders.

The weekday workflow starts at 10:47 PM IST / 8:17 PM Saudi time and skips NSE holidays from the existing calendar. The report arrives after it finishes, not exactly at the trigger minute. Keep the Dhan access token valid at night as well. GitHub scheduling delays and minutes quotas still apply. Preview sends nothing and uploads the CSV and summary as GitHub artifacts. Choose **Actions → Nightly F&O Paper Trading → Run workflow → preview** to test manually. Outside the weekday live windows, manual runs use the most recent completed trading day. Manual replay between 07:30 IST and market close is disabled on trading days to avoid rate-limit contention. Repeated manual sends can deliver the report again.

## The ten tests

- C1 CPR trend, C2 price vs CPR, C3 Camarilla, C4 EMA 9/21, C5 VWAP, C6 RSI, C7 Supertrend, C8 volume spike and candle direction, C9 NIFTY Tide. Each trades independently on a qualifying completed underlying-chart candle. An opposite confirmed direction exits it. Neutral/missing does not count as a reversal. C9 applies the same confirmed NIFTY market direction to all eligible underlyings. Treat its results as correlated, not independent evidence.
- C10 uses the existing scanner's **7 of 9** CE/PE threshold as the combined test, not a new 10-condition signal and not a strict 9-of-9 test. It enters on a new qualifying state or side change, with at most one C10 position per underlying at once. It does not use the alert engine's cooldown or score-tier reminders as extra entries. Target +20% and stop −10% are computed from the option entry premium, not the underlying. A traded one-minute high/low can trigger either level. The simulated exit uses the next traded *scheduled* minute's opening print, not a fabricated exact threshold fill. If both levels are touched in the same minute, their order is unknown and the row is unresolved.
- New entries from the confirmed 09:35 close through 15:10 IST. All remaining positions are scheduled to square off at 15:15 IST. Signals use underlying equity/index candles and completed NIFTY 15-minute candles for market tide. Only simulated fills and C10 risk rules use option premiums.

## Prices, contracts and limits

- Selects the nearest expiry **after** the replay date and its closest listed ATM strike, based on underlying signal close. Same-day expiries are intentionally excluded because the active-contract endpoint may no longer serve them at night. This is a prospective nightly test using the current master, not a survivorship-free historical backtest. Uses listed security IDs and lot sizes. Does not choose a substitute premium if data are unavailable.
- Entry and reversal/square-off exits use recorded Dhan one-minute traded OHLCV opens at or after the signal boundary (with positive minute volume). These are historical observations, not tick timestamps, bid/ask quotes, or broker fills. Missing entry, missing exit, missing C10 path, or unknown trigger order is reported, never filled with a guessed price. Partial underlying sessions are excluded, and the coverage artifact lists them.
- The CSV records the source, signal, contract, expiry, strike, lot size, recorded premiums, simulated time, computed levels, exit reason and one-lot gross P&L. Charges, slippage and net P&L are not known from candle data and are not represented as actual values. The ten columns of results are separate strategy experiments, not a combined portfolio. No capital, liquidity, or margin allocation is modeled.
- Existing intraday alert signals and Next-Day Desk rankings already use the underlying charts. The morning watchlist isn't an input to this replay and is not restricted to the morning top ten.
